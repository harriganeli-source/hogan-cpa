Litigation

MDL Litigation Support

Multi-District Litigation consolidates complex civil cases before a single federal judge for coordinated pretrial proceedings. To fund the shared work, the plaintiffs' firms that team up on litigation of this scale create a common benefit fund, financed by court-ordered assessments on the participating firms. Courts are increasingly appointing CPAs to handle the financial accounting for these funds, a new practice Sarah has been part of since 2019.

Common Benefit Fund Administration

Fiduciary management of the fund itself: receiving assessments, paying the litigation's expenses in accordance with the court's orders, and keeping the fund in full tax compliance.

Budgeting & Capital Calls

Regular financial updates to leadership, budgeting analysis, and three-to-six-month expense projections, so each capital call reflects what the litigation actually needs.

Time & Expense Reporting

Participating firms report their time and expenses by court order. Sarah tracks every submission and turns the raw data into clear reports for leadership and the court: hours by task and case, held and shared costs, and total fund spend to date.

Appointed by the Court

Sarah works for the court and is directed by the litigation's leadership. Most judges want the fund's accounting handled quietly, and that is how she runs it: in the background, present at hearings and case management conferences when the judge asks.
